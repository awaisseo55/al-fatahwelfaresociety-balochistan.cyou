Who we are

A volunteer-driven welfare society founded to stand beside the people of Balochistan.

Balochistan is Pakistan's largest province by area and one of its most under-served. Entire villages live without a school, a clinic, or a safe source of drinking water. Al Fateh Welfare Society Balochistan was formed by local volunteers to close that gap — one village, one family, one child at a time.

We work directly with community elders and local volunteers, so every rupee donated reaches the families who need it most. Our work is transparent, non-political and open to all, regardless of background.

What we do

Four focus areas, chosen by the communities we serve.

📚

Education Support

School fees, books, uniforms and stationery for children from low-income families, plus support for community learning centres.

💧

Clean Water

Hand pumps, water filtration units and water tanks for villages that rely on unsafe or distant water sources.

🩺

Health Camps

Free medical camps, medicines, and help with treatment and transport costs for patients who cannot afford care.

🍚

Food & Relief

Monthly ration packs for widows and orphans, Ramadan and Eid distributions, and emergency relief during floods and droughts.
